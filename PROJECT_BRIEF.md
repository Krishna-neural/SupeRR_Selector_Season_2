# SupeRR Selector Season 2 — Project Brief

## Official Challenge

Identify and rank the 10 most reliable batters and 10 most reliable bowlers under pressure using historical IPL and T20I data from 2022 onwards.

The official Round 1 problem statement is the primary authority.

## Primary Objectives

1. Develop a scientifically defensible definition of match pressure.
2. Measure batting performance under pressure against normal performance.
3. Measure bowling performance under pressure against normal performance.
4. Account for match context, sample size, and statistical uncertainty.
5. Generate two evidence-based top-10 rankings.
6. Produce a reproducible methodology report.

## Core Philosophy

- Data integrity comes first.
- Mathematical correctness comes before mathematical complexity.
- Cricket-specific scoring rules must be respected.
- Batting and bowling must have separate analytical methodologies.
- All important rankings must be explainable.
- Code must be clean, modular, and understandable.
- Never fabricate data or results.
- Never overwrite original datasets.
- Never implement unvalidated mathematical assumptions.

## Technical Direction

Python is the preferred primary language.

Choose supporting libraries only after examining the dataset and justifying their usefulness.

Prefer established, maintainable technologies.

## Development Protocol

Work in separate, verifiable phases:

1. Dataset audit.
2. Data engineering.
3. Pressure modelling.
4. Batting and bowling performance modelling.
5. Statistical reliability.
6. Player rankings.
7. Robustness experiments.
8. Methodology report.
9. Final quality assurance.

Do not skip phases.

## Approval Gates

Before moving to the next phase:

- Explain what was completed.
- Show generated files.
- Report tests and their results.
- Identify unresolved problems.
- Explain important mathematical decisions.
- Request review of the phase deliverables.

## Final Deliverables

- Top 10 batters.
- Top 10 bowlers.
- Complete supporting rankings.
- Mathematical methodology report.
- Supporting analytical evidence.
- Reproducible source code.

The official hackathon rules and problem statement override any conflicting instruction in this document.
